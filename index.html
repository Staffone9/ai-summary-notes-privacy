Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 2026

AI Summary Notes ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"), including AI-powered summarization features.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information (Optional): If you choose to sign in using Google, we may receive basic profile information such as your name and email address.
  • User Content: Text, audio, or documents that you choose to paste, upload, or summarize within the App.

  • Purchase Information: If you make a one-time in-app purchase (such as purchasing word credits), the transaction is processed by the app store. We do not receive or store your payment details.

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as contacts, messages, photos, call logs, or precise location.

2. AI Processing & User Consent

When you use AI summarization features, the content you submit is securely sent to a third-party AI service provider solely to generate the summary you requested.

  • Your content is used only for providing AI-powered summaries.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • By using AI features in the App, you consent to sending submitted content to a third-party AI service for processing.

3. How We Use Your Information

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with trusted third-party services strictly necessary to operate the App, such as authentication providers, AI processing services, and analytics or crash reporting tools.

5. Data Retention

  • User Content: Content submitted for summarization is not retained longer than necessary to provide the feature, unless you explicitly choose to save it within the App.
  • Usage Data: Diagnostic data may be retained in aggregated or anonymized form to improve reliability.

6. Third-Party Services

AI Summary Notes may use third-party services such as:
