Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 2026

AI Summary Notes ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Eligibility

This App is intended for users aged 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: If you choose to sign in using Google, we receive basic profile information such as your name and email address.
  • Usage & Diagnostics: Information such as app interactions, feature usage, crash logs, and performance diagnostics to improve app stability and user experience.
  • Purchase Information: If you make a one-time in-app purchase (such as purchasing word credits), the transaction is processed by the app store. We do not receive or store your payment details.

We do not collect or store user notes, summaries, uploaded documents, audio files, or any personal content created within the App.

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as contacts, messages, photos, call logs, or precise location.

3. AI Processing

When you use AI-powered summarization features, the text you submit is securely transmitted to a third-party AI service provider solely to generate the requested summary.

  • The submitted content is processed only to generate the summary.
  • We do not store, review, or retain the submitted content.
  • We do not use submitted content for advertising or marketing.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate users and manage accounts
  • To provide and improve app functionality
  • To manage one-time purchases and word credit balances
  • To monitor app performance and fix bugs
  • To respond to support requests

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with trusted third-party service providers strictly necessary to operate the App, such as:

  • Google Sign-In (authentication)
  • Analytics and crash reporting services
  • AI processing service providers
  • App store billing services

6. Data Retention

  • Account Information: Retained for as long as your account remains active or as required for legal or operational purposes.
  • Usage & Diagnostics: May be retained in aggregated or anonymized form to improve reliability and performance.

7. Account & Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

To submit a deletion request, please use the following link:

Account Deletion Request: Request account and data deletion

Requests are processed within 7–30 days in accordance with Google Play policies.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit, to protect your information. However, no system is completely secure.
